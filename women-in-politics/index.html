Data visualisation - Women in politics

An illustrated, storytelling-first infographic series celebrating the history and impact of women leaders in Indian politics.

Context
Academic project, NID Bengaluru, with a Noah collaboration
Time
2 months
Team
Team of 4
Tools
Figma, Procreate, Photoshop, Illustrator, Charticulator
Top 100Information is Beautiful Awards, 2023 and 2024
100+gender equality data sources analysed with Noah

In this project we gathered data, conducted research and subsequently developed information graphics to communicate insights on women's representation in Indian parliamentary politics.

The posters

Three A2 posters, one story

We told our data story through three A2 sized posters, with each poster dedicated to a specific theme.

I

History

History of women's representation in Lok Sabha.

II

Retrospective

Women in Lok Sabha: a retrospective.

III

Global comparison

Women's representation, country by country.

Format

Print publication · A2 foldable

It was decided that a printable format would work well for this project for ease of sharing. A large A2 poster print was designed that could be folded into an A4 booklet.

Poster 01

History

This first poster sets the context for the project. It explores the timeline of the women's reservation bill, imagines an information portrait of a woman politician and maps out a time series analysis of women's representation in different constituencies.

Poster 02

Retrospective

While the first poster served to visualize the reservation debate, this poster chronicles the career lines and backgrounds of women in parliament. It begins with a personal reading of women politicians and an understanding of their career trajectories, and moves to a quantitative narrative of women in parliament. On the reverse side of this poster is a visualization of all the 78 women in the 17th Lok Sabha.

Poster 03

Global comparison

In this poster, a global comparison of women's rights in the political space contextualizes the Indian position in terms of the milestones that have been achieved. It also establishes broader global patterns for women's representation in the political space.
