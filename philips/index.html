Healthcare service KPI dashboard

A single workspace for service leaders to see which KPIs are off target, why, and where to act, with AI-written summaries, drilldowns by zone, business unit and site, and an alert queue for equipment issues.

Tools
Figma, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts, Enterprise Design Systems, Claude Code

Live prototype

Try the product

The working prototype of the dashboard. Switch between the homebase, the twelve KPI drilldowns and the alerts queue; change the period, filter, sort and open an alert to see its details.
