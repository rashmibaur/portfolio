Introduction

Dashboards for Bosch Mobility India's marketing division, built on diverse datasets and brand guidelines, giving officials at every level clear, engaging insights for better decisions.

Challenges

  • Making intricate datasets clear and relevant.
  • Designing within development and technical limits.
  • Keeping visuals varied within strict brand guidelines.

Outcomes

  • Information categories shaped by user research.
  • Varied visualisations, consistent with the brand.
  • Detail-on-demand hierarchy for users at every level.

Intended audience

Upper management, department heads, product salespeople and analysts.

Research

User interviews

In-person interviews on how data is presented today, and where it hurts.

Department head

“The dashboards and visuals are currently developed ad-hoc, just before meetings.”

Product salesperson

“Data is hard to find. It lives in links that sometimes expire.”

Analyst

“Visuals are inconsistent, so comparing with past data is hard.”

Developer

“Some data repeats monthly or yearly. Fixed visuals for it would speed up development.”

    Group

  3. Ground rules

    Stay flexible

    • No set number of groups

    • Unclear cards go to the side

Synthesis

Insights

  • Overview

    Not consolidated

    Marketing data is not consolidated, so there is no overview.

  • Consistency

    Inconsistent visuals

    Visual styles and formats are inconsistent, which confuses users.

  • Findability

    Hard to find

    Users struggle to find the visuals for their department.

  • Structure

    No cyclic format

    There is no structured format for annual or monthly cyclic data.

  • Engagement

    Monotonous charts

    Dashboards rely on monotonous chart types, limiting engagement.

Personas

User personas

Personas built from the research insights.

Department Head

A senior department head with 15+ years in the automotive industry.

Goal

Stay on top of market trends, sales performance and competitors.

Pain point

Juggling many datasets across departments is slow and overwhelming.

Needs

A user-friendly dashboard with concise insights for his department.

Behaviour

Prefers clear, concise, on-brand visuals that are easy to interpret.

Product Salesperson

An ambitious urban sales professional with 5 years in automotive.

Goal

Access real-time sales, customer and market data to tailor his strategy.

Pain point

Cannot get relevant data fast enough to respond to the market.

Needs

Quick access to sales metrics, customer insights and competitors, visually clear.

Behaviour

Prefers intuitive, interactive dashboards he can drill into on any device.

Direction

Design propositions

Keep every visual on-brand and consistent.

Organise information in layers: detail on demand.

Use varied chart types that are readable and engaging.

Reflection

Learnings

    User research shaped how information is categorised.

    Dashboards stayed on-brand and still worked for users at every level.

  • Varied visuals help people understand
