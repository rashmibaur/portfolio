Context

skating

skateboarding

Each sport is pursued competitively or for leisure. Performance hinges on speed, timing and stamina, and competitions use set tracks with multiple laps.

Currently, how do skaters, cyclists and runners track their speed, timings and performance during practice?
  • Smart watch

    Real-time speed, distance and route, with instant feedback.

  • Mobile applications

    GPS records speed, distance, elevation and pace.

  • Speed & distance sensors

    Attach to equipment; data is analysed after practice.

  • Stopwatch

    Times sessions, intervals and repetitions.

  • Training log

    A written record of workouts and progress over time.

Problem statement

Problem statement

Skaters, runners, and cyclists face challenges in independently tracking their technical data, relying on manual performance tracking methods to identify areas for improvement.

Competitor analysis

Finding competitors' strengths and weaknesses, to offer what they don't and fix pain points they overlook.

Social fitness platform for cyclists, runners and skaters: track, analyse and share workouts.

Features

  • Social networking
  • Training plans
  • Challenges
  • Premium analytics

Strengths

  • Large library of user routes and segments

Weaknesses

Garmin

Garmin Connect: tracking and analysis for Garmin's GPS wearables, across many sports.

Features

  • Device compatibility
  • Performance metrics
  • Training plans
  • Insights & analysis
  • Challenges
  • Community

Strengths

  • Works with a wide range of devices
  • Strong training planning and analysis
  • Active, supportive community

Weaknesses

  • Can feel complex for casual users
  • Some features need a premium plan or extra purchases
  • Occasional sync and compatibility issues

App naming

kinetrack=kinetic+track
Kinetic
Generally refers to motion or movement.
Track
In sports like running or cycling, a track can refer to the oval-shaped path or course on which races or competitions take place.
Track
Can mean to monitor or follow the progress.
Track
Can mean to maintain a record or documentation.

+ =
