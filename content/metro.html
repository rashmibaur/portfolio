The product

A decision platform for new footwear launches

The New Product Introduction (NPI) Intelligence Platform is designed for fashion retail merchandising and assortment planning. It enables faster, more confident Go/No-Go and launch decisions for new footwear SKUs by leveraging integrated trend, product, and demand intelligence.

Why this matters

Business context

A footwear brand launches a high volume of new footwear SKUs every season across categories, brands, and price bands. The existing decision-making process presents significant challenges that directly impact business performance.

Current challenges

Decisions are primarily driven by intuition, fragmented reports, and late signals rather than integrated intelligence.

Missing visibility

There is no unified view of trend momentum, comparable historical performance, or demand confidence to support early decisions.

High-risk outcomes

This leads to over-launching weak products, running pilots even when confidence is already high, and carrying excess inventory.

Why NPI matters: improving launch success rates, reducing unsold inventory, and increasing confidence in assortment decisions.

Approach

Design principles

Confidence over complexity

Surface clear, actionable signals rather than overwhelming users with raw data. Reduce cognitive load so decisions come easier.

Explain the “why,” not just the “what”

Every recommendation should surface the reasoning behind it. Teams need context to agree, challenge, or override with confidence.

Early signals over delayed certainty

Act on directional intelligence in the moment – waiting for perfect data costs speed and competitive advantage.

Progressive disclosure for heavy data

Lead with the summary. Let users drill deeper only when they need it, keeping the surface clean without hiding important detail.

Human–AI interaction design

AI informs, humans decide

AI does the analytical heavy lifting. The UX ensures every output is transparent, auditable, and always subject to human judgement.

UX responsibilities

  • Show confidence levels, not false certainty
  • Make AI outputs fully auditable and traceable
  • Allow human override with captured context

The goal is augmented decision-making – AI informs, humans decide.

What AI does

  • Extracts product attributes automatically
  • Identifies similar SKUs and analogues
  • Predicts demand across scenarios

Framework

NPI journey

Trend intelligence

Decisions enabled
  • Which trends to invest in early
  • Which styles to deprioritize or ignore
  • Where to focus design exploration
Metrics & signals
  • Trend velocity (rate of growth)
  • Engagement momentum (likes, mentions, shares)
  • Trend saturation vs emergence score
  • Attribute popularity index (color, silhouette, material)
  • Global vs regional trend relevance
Value added
  • Reduces time spent on manual trend research
  • Shifts inspiration from subjective to evidence-based
  • Enables early access to high-potential trends before market saturation

Product iteration (design + validation)

Decisions enabled
  • Which designs to iterate further
  • Which samples to stop before procurement
  • Which products are ready for pilot testing
Metrics & signals
  • Similarity index to successful past products
  • Attribute-level historical performance
  • Predicted success probability
  • Sample-to-pilot conversion likelihood
Value added
  • Improves quality of shortlisted products
  • Reduces sampling and vendor procurement costs
  • Increases likelihood of pilot success
  • Enables faster design-to-decision cycles

Demand forecasting & launch planning

Decisions enabled
  • Pilot quantity and store allocation
  • Go / no-go scale decisions
  • SKU promotion or discontinuation
  • Timing and sequencing of launch
Metrics & signals
  • Forecasted demand by region, store type, and season
  • Expected sell-through rate
  • Pilot quantity efficiency
  • Quantities of SKU & product category mix
Value added
  • Reduces pilot-phase cost and inventory risk
  • Improves launch efficiency and revenue realization
  • Shortens feedback loops from pilot to SKU
  • Enables data-backed, confidence-driven launches

Reflection

Key learning

Structure prompts like system instructions

Role, scope, and constraints outperform descriptive prose.

Use tokens for precision, not verbosity

Concise, component-level language reduces waste and improves output fidelity.

Always refine manually at the end

Human review ensures consistency, scale, and decision quality.

AI accelerated early exploration and structuring, while human intervention ensured precision, consistency, and decision quality.
