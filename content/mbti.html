What is MBTI?

The Myers-Briggs Type Indicator is a popular personality tool by Katharine Cook Briggs and Isabel Briggs Myers, based on the theories of Carl Jung. It helps people understand their own preferences and how they relate to others.

Four questions, each with two sides

EExtraversionpeople & activity
Where do you get your energy?
IntroversionIsolitude & inner thoughts
SSensingfacts & details
How do you take in information?
IntuitionNpatterns & the big picture
TThinkinglogic & objective analysis
How do you make decisions?
FeelingFemotions & personal values
JJudgingstructure & closure
How do you organise your world?
PerceivingPflexibility & open-endedness

Your side on each question gives one of 16 types, such as INTJ or ESFP.

Where can someone take the test?

  • Online · Free

    16Personalities.com

    A free MBTI-like test with detailed descriptions of each type.

  • Online · Paid

    MBTIOnline.com

    The official Myers-Briggs Company site, with a detailed report after the test.

  • Online · Free

    Truity.com

    A free MBTI-like test with explanations of the types.

  • In person

    Licensed practitioner

    A practitioner or psychologist administers and interprets the test and gives personal feedback. Find one online or at local counselling centres.

  • In person

    Workshops & training

    Some organisations and development workshops include the MBTI in their programmes.

Critique

Drawbacks & limitations

  • Lack of scientific validity

    Little consistent empirical support. Many psychologists doubt it is a reliable assessment.

  • Binary categories

    Sixteen types from four either/or choices oversimplify a personality that sits on a continuum.

  • Reliability

    People who retake the test can get different results, which undermines its credibility.

  • Inflexibility

    It implies a fixed type, although personality changes with experience and growth.

  • Social desirability bias

    Answers can reflect how people see themselves, or want to be seen, which gives inaccurate results.
