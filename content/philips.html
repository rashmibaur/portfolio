Every hour of unplanned equipment downtime delays diagnoses. The goal was one cockpit where service leaders see what is off target, why it is happening, and what to do about it.

Objective and introduction

Keeping diagnostic equipment up

Hospitals depend on high-uptime diagnostic equipment: MRI scanners, CT systems, ultrasound units and digital radiography. When one goes down, the damage spreads.

What unplanned downtime sets off

  1. 01Equipment stopsUnplanned downtime, mid-schedule.
  2. 02Diagnostics delayedScans wait, and so do patients.
  3. 03Surgery backs upEmergency workflows stall.
  4. 04SLA penaltiesThe cost lands on the manufacturer.

Why service health was hard to see

1
Serviced under contractDedicated field maintenance networks keep equipment running across global healthcare systems.
2
Data in silosIncident logs, telemetry alerts, dispatch schedules and satisfaction scores lived in ServiceNow, ERP systems and cloud telemetry.
3
Manual correlationRegional directors, service heads and account managers stitched spreadsheets and tickets together by hand.

Project vision

One centralized B2B intelligence cockpit, with structured AI root cause analysis in place of manual spreadsheet work.

Organising the KPIs

Two families, four shapes of data

The 12 KPIs are all predefined from operational telemetry and ServiceNow logs. They split into two families by who feels the problem, and into four archetypes by how the data behaves.

Higher or lower is better

AI

AI-based root cause analysis

In high-stakes enterprise work, open-ended chatbots add friction: operators need structure now, not a conversation. The platform puts structured, deterministic AI directly in the cockpit, bridging diagnosis and remediation.

How an insight is made

  1. 01IngestTelemetry, ServiceNow incidents, shift logs and SLAs.
  2. 02DetectFinds metric breaches across the rolling period, such as 28 days.
  3. 03ExplainSorts the failure into a risk archetype with clear drivers.
  4. 04PrescribeTurns it into a Where, Key metrics, Action card.
