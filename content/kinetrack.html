Each sport is pursued competitively or for leisure. Performance hinges on speed, timing and stamina, and competitions use set tracks with multiple laps.

Currently, how do skaters, cyclists and runners track their speed, timings and performance during practice?
  • Smart watch

    Real-time speed, distance and route, with instant feedback.

  • Mobile applications

    GPS records speed, distance, elevation and pace.

  • Speed & distance sensors

    Attach to equipment; data is analysed after practice.

  • Stopwatch

    Times sessions, intervals and repetitions.

  • Training log

    A written record of workouts and progress over time.
