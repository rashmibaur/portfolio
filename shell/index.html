Brief

Objective & challenges

B2B card portfolios face a silent threat: customer churn that builds gradually, often going undetected until it is too late to act. Account managers need to identify risk early, understand why customers are disengaging, and act with confidence, all within the constraints of their day-to-day workload.

Who is at risk?

Ranked, prioritised customer lists.

Why might they churn?

Diagnostic drivers and root causes.

What actions to take?

Clear, recommended next steps.

Did the actions work?

Outcome tracking and impact metrics.

Objective: design Power BI dashboards that translate churn prediction model outputs into clear, actionable insights, enabling proactive retention and measurable revenue protection.

Responsibilities

My role

End-to-end UX leadership

Led end-to-end UX design for the AI-powered churn dashboard in Power BI, including user research with EU account managers to understand workflows, pain points, and decision-making needs.

Data synthesis & collaboration

Synthesized 1,000+ data points from qualitative and quantitative research. Partnered with data analysts to align design decisions with data dictionaries and model outputs, translating complex data into intuitive interfaces.

Design & implementation

Owned information architecture, wireframing, interaction design, and final dashboard development. All designs were crafted within Power BI's constraints and aligned to brand guidelines.

Measurable impact & success

Reduced decision-making time by 80%, prevented 40% of at-risk churn, and scaled to 100+ managers. Achieved 10/10 NPS, reflecting strong adoption and user trust.

Primary user

Account manager (AM)

A strategic business-facing role responsible for maintaining customer relationships, improving margins, driving billed volume, and preventing customer churn.

01

Relationship management

Maintains portfolio health, handles complaints, and renegotiates commercial terms with corporate card clients.

02

Revenue protection

Prevents churn, retains high-value accounts, and identifies upsell and cross-sell opportunities.

03

Cross-team collaboration

Works closely with Finance, Credit, Sales, and Marketing to diagnose churn and execute retention strategies.

Primary goal
“Take actions to prevent churn effectively and confidently.”

The AM needs quick, reliable insights that answer:

  1. Who is at risk
  2. Why they might churn
  3. What actions to take
  4. Whether those actions worked
  5. How the portfolio is performing overall

Stakeholders

The AM's ecosystem

Account managers do not operate in isolation. Diagnosing churn and executing retention strategies requires active co-ordination across the business.

  • Marketing teamAligns on campaign plans, targeting logic, and performance outcomes.

  • CustomerDirect engagement for health checks, complaint resolution, and root-cause diagnosis.

The model

Understanding the churn model

The churn prediction model forms the backbone of the dashboard system, providing data-driven insights to power account manager decisions.

01.

Learns from historical customer data

The model analyzes past customer behavior patterns and historical trends.

02.

Predicts customers at risk

Identifies which customers are likely to churn based on data signals.

03.

Identifies top drivers causing churn

Surfaces the key factors and reasons contributing to churn risk.

04.

Tracks outcomes vs actions

Measures business impact by comparing predictions with actual results.

Needs

Detailed user needs breakdown

User needs were structured into three distinct levels, from portfolio-wide awareness down to individual customer action.

01

Strategic

Portfolio-level

  • Portfolio health: margins, volumes, geography, business type
  • Top revenue customers vs high-risk customers
  • Ongoing campaign performance
  • Saved vs lost volumes over time
  • Energy transition / fuel mix indicators (e.g., EV share)
02

Diagnostic

Customer-level

  • Customer risk score & model confidence
  • Root-cause analysis: reasons & patterns
  • Transaction history & usage patterns
  • Past performance & touchpoint history
  • Campaign logs (emails sent / bounced / opened)
03

Operational

Action-oriented

  • Prioritisation of customers by risk and value
  • Quick recommended actions
  • Visibility of action impact (saved volume & margin)
  • Smooth navigation across portfolio → segment → customer
  • Reduced manual searching time across systems

Final dashboards

Churn insights tab

01

Model dependability metrics

Displays model confidence score, recall, actual vs predicted saved/lost volumes.

Users glance at these KPIs to judge whether they can rely on the model's suggestions before taking actions.

02

Immediate-risk overview

Shows customers who need urgent attention based on predicted churn risk and volume drop.

Users scan the risk-sorted list first, identify top red-flag clients, and prioritize their daily outreach.

03

AI Q&A assistant

Conversational UI to ask questions like “Why is this customer dropping?” or “Show EV customers only.”

Users type quick questions to clarify metrics, avoid manual filtering, and speed up their analysis.

04

Customer-level drill-down

Clicking a customer reveals diagnostic drivers (e.g., credit rating, last transaction, dispute recency).

Users quickly hop into individual cases to understand why the customer is at risk before calling or emailing them.

Final dashboards

Portfolio overview tab

01

Regional volume distribution

Shows how volumes are distributed across geographies.

Users compare markets to see which regions need targeted interventions.

02

Universal filters

Filters on the left let users slice data by CPA, legal entity, fuel type, region, customer segment, date, etc.

Users frequently adjust filters to explore patterns, for example checking churn risk only for EV-dominant customers or specific regions.

03

Top customers

Table listing high-value customers by volume and margin.

Users quickly skim top contributors and investigate any showing negative trends.

04

Portfolio-level snapshot

Shows predicted vs actual saved/lost volumes, margins, and portfolio performance.

Users review this daily/weekly to assess whether the portfolio is stabilizing or declining.

05

Decarbonisation journey

Shows movement from traditional fuels to EV/CNG/LNG over 12 months.

Users monitor shifts in fuel mix to identify upsell opportunities or declining categories.

Final dashboards

Customer view tab

01

Risk assessment panel

Shows whether the selected customer/group is at risk of churn, with red/amber/green indicators.

Users validate which customers need immediate action versus routine monitoring.

02

Customer-group filtering

Selecting a customer group loads all KPIs and visuals for that segment.

Users filter by account type or segment to review specific clients before meetings or interventions.

03

Text-based reasoning & recommended actions

AI-generated explanations for churn risk and recommended next steps.

Users read the reasoning to prepare talking points for client conversations and follow the suggested actions.

04

Comprehensive customer metrics

Displays volume, margin, product mix, fuel usage split, margins by product, and transaction history.

Users analyze consumption patterns to uncover drops, shifts, or anomalies.

05

Card-level activity

Shows active vs transacting cards and monthly card usage.

Users look for sudden drops in card activity, a major churn signal.

Final dashboards

Campaigns tab

01

Customer list with volume & margin

Table listing all customers engaged in campaigns, along with their recent volumes and margins.

Users click into customers with low engagement or unexpected loss to focus follow-up actions.

02

Email performance insights

Displays email bounce rates, non-delivery, and engagement levels.

Users diagnose deliverability issues and coordinate with marketing/CRM teams to fix email quality problems.

03

Volume saved through campaigns

Highlights how much volume was preserved due to campaign interventions.

Users evaluate ROI and justify campaign continuation or scaling.

04

Stage-wise conversion tracking

Funnel-style visual showing performance at each phase.

Users identify which stage has maximum drop-off and adjust messaging or tactics accordingly.
